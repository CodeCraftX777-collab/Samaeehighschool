About Us

Building Bright Futures in Washim District

Samaee High School & Jr. College, Wadap is a premier English medium institution offering quality education from Nursery to 12th Standard. Under Samaee Mahila Va Balkalyan Bahuddeshiya Shikshan Sanstha, Akola.

Affiliated with Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University, recognized by Govt. of Maharashtra, following NEP 2020 curriculum.
